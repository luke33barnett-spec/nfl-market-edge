# Portfolio description

NFL Market Edge is an NFL market analytics dashboard built with JavaScript, Node.js, GitHub Actions, and Vercel. It combines scheduled odds collection, source-specific betting-split imports, explainable ranking, regression forecasts, and reproducible historical experiments.

## Suggested résumé bullets

- Built an NFL analytics dashboard with scheduled odds collection, timestamp validation, explainable market signals, and automated GitHub-to-Vercel deployment configuration.
- Implemented a dependency-free ridge regression and chronological validation on 816 NFL games from 2023–2025, reducing scoring-margin MAE from 11.09 to 10.26 points versus a constant home-field baseline.
- Developed a reproducible historical strategy experiment with separate discovery/evaluation periods, actual recorded betting prices, push handling, drawdown reporting, and weekly block-bootstrap uncertainty; documented why the results did not establish a profitable edge.

Use the deployment bullet after activating and verifying your own GitHub/Vercel workflow. The other bullets describe work verified locally. Do not claim live sharp-money identification, profitable betting, or model calibration: those have not been established.

## Interview discussion

Explain why random train/test splits cause leakage; why higher prediction accuracy does not guarantee positive betting returns; why source-specific public splits are imperfect evidence of professional action; why sample size and multiple testing matter; and how free-tier constraints shaped the static dashboard architecture.
