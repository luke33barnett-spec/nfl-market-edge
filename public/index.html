METHOD / TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

What’s behind the ranking

The evidence, assumptions, and limits are part of the product.

01

Observe the market

Collect spreads and American odds twice a day. Compare each sportsbook with its own first recorded quote. A lower spread for a team means the market moved toward that team.

Example: a favorite moving from −3.5 to −2.5 is movement toward its opponent. The opponent’s +3.5 may no longer be available.

02

Look for confirmation

Reverse movement requires at least two books moving toward a side with 40% or less of reported spread tickets. Movement against money applies the same rule to reported dollars.

A money-minus-ticket gap indicates larger average wagers. It does not prove those wagers came from professionals. Splits describe their named source, not the whole betting market.

03

Estimate, then compare

A ridge regression predicts home scoring margin using pregame Elo, recent eight-game scoring and defense, home field, and rest. Features only use earlier completed games.

Historical holdout residuals approximate the margin distribution. Integer margins allow pushes. These probabilities are experimental and have not been validated as calibrated betting probabilities.

04

Keep the claim narrow

Default ranking: 60% sharp-action evidence, 25% estimated model edge, 15% available line and price. The sharp score combines multi-book movement, reverse movement, movement against money, and the money-ticket gap.

These are hand-selected weights. A review candidate needs reverse movement, sharp score ≥45, estimated EV >2.5%, at least 200 holdout residuals, and a future kickoff. This is a screening rule, not a proven strategy.

Data and refresh policy

Live quotes older than 18 hours are excluded; public splits older than 24 hours are excluded. The whole live board becomes stale after 18 hours. The twice-daily collection can miss fast market moves. We do not claim to identify steam bettors or which book led a move.

Injuries and weather are not model inputs in this version. They can explain apparent contrarian movement. Early-season team form is sparse; international games do not yet receive a reliable neutral-site adjustment in live forecasts.

Free mode uses The Odds API’s free quota, public nflverse results, GitHub Actions, and static Vercel hosting. No paid database, LLM, or scraping service is required. Public splits are manually imported from sources you are permitted to use.

Import format
